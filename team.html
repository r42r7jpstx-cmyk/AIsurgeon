Leadership & Team

Frank Lee

Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Frank Lee brings over a decade of cross-functional experience spanning operations, finance, and technology startups. Originally from Suzhou, China, he has lived and worked in the United States for more than 11 years and is currently based in Seattle.

He began his career in San Francisco at a professional accounting firm, serving as an Operations Manager with a focus on organizational management, compliance processes, and business operations.

Frank later transitioned into the technology sector and currently serves as the Chief Operating Officer of an AI startup. At NeuroForge Medical, he leads operational strategy, corporate development, and organizational scaling.
