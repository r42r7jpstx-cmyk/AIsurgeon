Compliance & Safety

NeuroForge Medical develops AI-driven software systems intended to assist in the control and optimization of precision surgical instruments. Our technology is designed to support clinical workflows while maintaining human oversight at all times.

All NeuroForge systems are architected with surgeon-in-the-loop principles, ensuring that final clinical decisions and control authority remain with qualified medical professionals.

NeuroForge Medical is committed to responsible development aligned with applicable medical device regulations and clinical standards. Our solutions are not intended to provide autonomous clinical decision-making or medical advice.
