Software-Defined Intelligence for Precision Surgery

NeuroForge Medical develops AI-driven software systems that enable programmable control of precision surgical instruments, supporting safer and more consistent surgical workflows.

What We Do

We build software-defined intelligence that bridges artificial intelligence and advanced surgical hardware. Our systems translate surgical intent into controlled, repeatable device behavior—while maintaining human oversight at every stage.

Why It Matters

Modern surgery demands extreme precision and reliability. By applying AI-assisted programming to surgical device control, NeuroForge Medical aims to reduce variability, enhance safety, and support better clinical outcomes.
